Advancing Research Through Innovation

ICAICT 2027 provides an international platform for researchers, students, academicians and industry professionals to present innovative research and exchange ideas.

About ICAICT 2027

The IEEE International Conference on Artificial Intelligence, Computing and Technology focuses on emerging developments across Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Internet of Things, Cyber Security, Cloud Computing and related technologies.

The conference encourages high-quality research papers, technical presentations, demonstrations and discussions between academia and industry.
